Dose-Response Analysis for the Evolution Experiment

By Alyssah Mack and Saaz Sakrikar

Fluconazole (FLC) growth curve dose-response analyses used to determine the drug concentration at which the evolution experiment would be conducted. Each report fits an IC50 dose-response model to growth curve data (area under the curve) across a panel of FLC concentrations, comparing an alternate media condition to the YPD control.

About this analysis

Growth curves were collected for yeast strains across a range of FLC concentrations (0–64 μg/mL) in two media backgrounds (SCGlu and YPGly), each compared against a YPD control, and read at two timepoints (24 hours and 72 hours). For each combination, growth (area under the growth curve, auc_l) was normalized to the maximum observed response per condition, and a four-parameter log-logistic (LL.4) dose-response model was fit using the drc R package to estimate the EC50 / IC50 — the concentration data combined from batches collected July 2–July 20. The strains grew slower in YPGlycerol than in SCGlucose, which is why the area under the curve was used as opposed to the growth rate of each. Meaningful data could not be obtained for the strains growing in YPGlycerol on a plot of FLC concentration against growth rate. Because the strains in SCGlucose reached stationary phase in 24 hours, the growth was analyzed at both 24 and 72 hour timepoints.
